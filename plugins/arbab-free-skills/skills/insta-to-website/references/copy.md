# Writing the two languages

## Arabic (first)
- Natural, warm, Emirati/Gulf-friendly Modern Arabic: readable by everyone in the UAE, with light local touches where they fit the brand voice (<bdi dir="rtl">«احجز موعدك»</bdi>، <bdi dir="rtl">«كلّمنا على الواتساب»</bdi>، <bdi dir="rtl">«تعال جرّب»</bdi>، <bdi dir="rtl">«حيّاكم»</bdi>). For formal brands (clinics, law) stay in clean MSA with a friendly tone.
- Short sentences. One idea per line. Verbs first.
- Keep their own words: if the bio has a line, reuse it.
- Never translate word by word from English. Write the Arabic first, then the English.
- Avoid machine phrases (say the concrete thing instead):

<bdi dir="rtl">«نحن نقدم لكم أفضل الخدمات»</bdi>، <bdi dir="rtl">«في عالم اليوم»</bdi>، <bdi dir="rtl">«حلول مبتكرة ومتكاملة»</bdi>، <bdi dir="rtl">«لا تتردد في التواصل معنا»</bdi>، <bdi dir="rtl">«نسعى جاهدين»</bdi>

- Numbers: keep Western digits (1, 2, 3) as most UAE brands do on Instagram, unless their posts use Arabic-Indic.
- Brand names stay as they write them (Latin names can stay Latin inside Arabic text).

## English
- Plain, confident, short. No "unleash", "elevate", "seamless", "world-class", "cutting-edge", "one-stop".
- Sentence case headings.

## Microcopy that works
| Use | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp button | <div dir="rtl" align="right">‏كلّمنا واتساب</div> | Chat on WhatsApp |
| Booking | <div dir="rtl" align="right">‏احجز موعدك</div> | Book a visit |
| Directions | <div dir="rtl" align="right">‏الموقع على الخريطة</div> | Get directions |
| Instagram | <div dir="rtl" align="right">‏تابعنا على إنستغرام</div> | Follow on Instagram |
| Language toggle | English | <div dir="rtl" align="right">‏عربي</div> |
| Pre-filled WhatsApp text | <div dir="rtl" align="right">‏هلا، شفت موقعكم وأبي أستفسر</div> | Hi, I saw your website |

## Slogan proposals (when none exists)
Three options, each Arabic + English, in their voice: one about the result for the customer, one about the craft, one short punchy line (3-4 words). Mark your pick and why.
