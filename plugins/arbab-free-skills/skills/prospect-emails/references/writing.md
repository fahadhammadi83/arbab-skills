# Writing the emails

Each email must read like the sender typed it for that one company this morning. Mail filters and people both notice templates.

## Every email
- **Plain text,** 60 to 120 words. No HTML, images, attachments, tracking or links.
- **Address the company team** ("Hi Lina Beauty team"), never a person by name.
- **Open on their specific gap,** as something the sender noticed on their site. Not a compliment, not "I hope this finds you well".
- **One or two lines on what the sender does about it.** Concrete, no buzzwords.
- **A small free next step and a question.** For example "Happy to send a free 2-minute check of your site. Who's the right person for this?"
- **No price.** It comes after they reply.
- **No claims you can't prove:** clients, results, "the best in the city".
- **Sign-off on its own lines:** name / company / website / phone (if the user wants it).

## Across the batch: genuinely different
- Vary the opening, the order of the parts, the sentence lengths and the closing question.
- No sentence repeats from one email to the next. The `check` command rejects two bodies more than 60% alike.
- **Subjects:** every one different, short and specific to that company. No clickbait, ALL CAPS, "Re:" or emoji.

## Language
- Write in the company's language. An Arabic-first site gets Arabic: natural Gulf Arabic as a person writes it, not a translation.
- English emails are plain and warm, with short sentences.

## Follow-ups (touch 2 on day 5, touch 3 on day 14)
- **Touch 2:** reply in the same thread with **one new thing**: another gap you noticed, a quick idea, or a one-line example. Under 70 words.
- **Touch 3:** a two-line close-out. "I'll leave it here. If it's useful later, just reply to this email." Then stop for good.
- A follow-up may include one link if it helps, for example a free check you made for them.

## Example (fictional company)
**Subject:** Arabic booking page for Lina Beauty?

Hi Lina Beauty team,

I was looking at your site and noticed it's only in English, while most of your Google reviews are in Arabic. Clients who search in Arabic may not find you, or may leave before booking.

We build simple bilingual pages with WhatsApp booking for salons in Dubai, usually live within a week.

Happy to send a free 2-minute check of your site with three quick fixes. Who would be the right person to send it to?
