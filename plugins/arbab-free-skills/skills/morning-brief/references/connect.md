# Set it up once (3 steps, about 3 minutes)

Menus in Claude can move. If a name looks different, look for the same words.

## 1. Add the skill
Claude on the web → **Customize → Skills** → upload `morning-brief.zip` → switch it on.
(If asked, switch on **code execution** in the same settings.)

## 2. Connect your email and calendar
**Customize → Connectors** → find **Gmail** and **Google Calendar**, or **Microsoft 365** (Outlook mail and calendar) → **Connect** → sign in → allow.
A work account may need your IT team's approval the first time.

## 3. Make your morning project
**Projects → New project** → name it <bdi dir="rtl">«موجزي الصباحي»</bdi>.
Optional: add a three-line note saying who matters most to you (your manager, your top clients, your family). The brief ranks your day around them.

## Then, every morning
Open the project and type:

<div dir="rtl" align="right">

‏سو لي موجز اليوم

</div>

## What Claude can and can't do with your email
It reads today's calendar and the recent inbox to plan your day. It never sends, replies, deletes, moves, accepts or declines anything. The replies it writes are drafts on the page; you send them yourself. You can disconnect any connector at any time from the same Connectors page.

## No connection?
It still works: drop a screenshot of today's calendar and one of your inbox, and type the same line.
