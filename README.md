# Arbab Skills

**Free, ready-to-use Claude skills from Arbab AI Technologies, Dubai.**
Each skill does a real job in one line. You point it at something you already have (screenshots, a photo, a transcript, your inbox) and get a finished result back. No prompts to copy, no homework.

## The skills

| Skill | What you give it | What you get back |
|---|---|---|
| **insta-to-website** | Screenshots of a business's Instagram | A stylish bilingual (Arabic + English) one-page website |
| **mandoob** · مندوبك | Your situation (visa, job, emirate) | A UAE PRO officer's case file: which visa, free zone or mainland, every document, official links |
| **whatsapp-replies** | A photo of your price list or menu | Ready WhatsApp Business replies in Emirati Arabic: greeting, away message, quick replies |
| **meeting-notes** | Any transcript, notes or chat screenshots | Finished minutes with decisions and action points |
| **morning-brief** | Your inbox and calendar (connected) | A one-page plan for your day |
| **weekly-report** | Your sent emails and meetings (connected) | A weekly report for your manager, no typing |
| **prospect-scan** | A list of businesses (CSV, Google Maps export or URLs) and what you sell | The 5 worth contacting today, each with its real website gap and a ready first message. Can run every night |

The morning-brief and weekly-report skills work best with Gmail or Outlook and your calendar connected in Claude (paid Claude plans). Without connectors, they work from what you paste or upload.

## Install

**Claude Code** (two lines):

```
/plugin marketplace add fahadhammadi83/arbab-skills
/plugin install arbab-free-skills@arbab-skills
```

**claude.ai** (desktop or web):
1. Download the skill's zip from the [`downloads`](downloads) folder.
2. In Claude, open **Settings → Capabilities → Skills** and upload the zip.
3. Ask for it by name, for example: "use insta-to-website on these screenshots".
